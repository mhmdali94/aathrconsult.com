# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two readers share every decision, and the site must convince both:

- **Business owners and general managers** of small and mid-sized companies. Not technical. They decide on trust and cost, and want to know the partner will be there next year.
- **IT managers** who evaluate the proposal. They look for specifics: which stack (Odoo, self-hosted servers, open-source email and files), where the data lives, how security is handled.

Primary market is **Saudi Arabia**, with Egypt second. The site is Arabic-first (RTL) with an English toggle; both languages are first-class, not translations bolted on.

## Product Purpose

Aathr Consulting (آثر للاستشارات) sells IT services built on open-source software with no recurring licence subscriptions: cloud and servers, cybersecurity, email and shared files, open-source ERP (Odoo), websites and online stores, IP telephony and cameras, and consulting. Deployed on the client's servers or Aathr's.

The site exists to turn a visitor into a **booked free consultation**. Success is a filled contact form from a qualified Saudi business. Everything else on the page (services, case work, FAQ, how-we-work) serves that one action.

Tagline: **أثرٌ يبقى / Impact that stays.**

## Brand Personality

**Trustworthy, steady, warm, local.**

- Trustworthy and steady: a calm, established partner. Low risk. Plain claims backed by real work, never hype.
- Warm and local: speaks the client's language naturally, Saudi and Arab identity visible, approachable like a person you can call, not a faceless vendor.

Voice: direct, respectful, confident without boasting. Explains technical things in business terms for owners, without hiding the detail IT managers need.

**Brand colors are fixed.** Obsidian navy (`#0A2540`) and royal champagne gold (`#D4AF37`), with the existing navy and gold scales in `style.css`, stay exactly as they are. Design work may change how much and where they are used, never the colors themselves.

## Anti-references

- **Old-school IT company sites**: clip-art servers and network cables, cluttered pages, stacked banners, dated corporate stock photos, walls of service bullet points.
- By extension, avoid anything that signals "cheap vendor" even though the offer is cost-effective: low cost must read as smart, not low quality.

## Design Principles

1. **Earn trust before asking.** Show real work, real process, and plain answers before the consultation CTA; the ask should feel like the natural next step.
2. **Speak to both readers at once.** Lead with the business outcome for the owner, keep the technical specifics one step away for the IT manager.
3. **Local by default.** Arabic is the primary design, not a mirrored afterthought; Saudi context shapes examples, tone, and imagery.
4. **Calm confidence.** Steadiness over spectacle: restraint, clarity, and consistency signal a partner who will still be around next year.
5. **Practice what we sell.** A fast, accessible, well-built site is itself proof of the service.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: text contrast, visible focus, full keyboard use, meaningful alt text.
- RTL and LTR must both be correct: layout direction, icons that imply direction, number and punctuation handling.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all reveal and scroll animations.
- Never rely on color alone to carry meaning.
